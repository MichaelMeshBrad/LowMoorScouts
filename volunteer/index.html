Help Young People Gain Skills for Life

Join the adult volunteer team at 20th Bradford South Scout Group. No previous Scouting experience is needed, and volunteering can be shaped around the time you have available.

Become a Volunteer

Volunteer With Us

Our volunteers make Scouting possible. They help young people enjoy new experiences, build confidence, make friends and learn practical skills in a safe and welcoming environment.

No Experience Needed

You do not need to have been a Scout or be an outdoor expert. Training, guidance and support are provided.

Flexible Roles

Help every week, support occasional activities or assist behind the scenes. We can find a role that suits your availability.

Make a Difference

Your time and skills can give local young people opportunities, adventures and experiences they may remember for life.

Ways You Can Help

There are many ways to support the group, both during section meetings and behind the scenes.

  • Help run activities at weekly section meetings
  • Support camps, trips and outdoor activities
  • Share a hobby, profession or practical skill
  • Help with administration, records or finances
  • Support fundraising and community events
  • Help maintain our Scout headquarters and grounds
  • Assist with communications, IT or social media
  • Join the Trustee Board and help oversee the group

What You Will Gain

New Skills

Build leadership, communication, teamwork and organisational skills through practical experience and training.

New Friends

Become part of a friendly local team and meet people from across the Scouting community.

Rewarding Experiences

See young people grow in confidence and take pride in the part you played in their development.

Safe and Supported

All adult volunteers receive appropriate induction, training and ongoing support. Roles involving young people are subject to the required safeguarding and disclosure checks in accordance with The Scouts' policies.

Interested in Volunteering?

Get in touch for an informal conversation. There is no obligation. We can explain the roles available and help you find something that suits your interests and availability.
